# OTT Content Protection

## Objective

Monitor digital platforms for unauthorized distribution of premium video-on-demand content.

## Investigation Workflow

1. Detect unauthorized content availability.
2. Verify infringement.
3. Track hosting sources.
4. Document evidence.
5. Support enforcement activities.

## Key Activities

- OTT monitoring
- Domain tracking
- Content verification
- Evidence collection
- Violation documentation

## Skills Demonstrated

- Content Protection
- Digital Rights Enforcement
- OSINT Investigation
- Domain Analysis
